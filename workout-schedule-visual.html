DAY 01

Upper — Strength

Mon

Heavy compound lifts. Move real weight for lower reps. This is the day you build the shirt-filling muscle: chest, back, shoulders.

Warm-Up5 min row or bike · arm circles · band pull-aparts 2×15 · empty bar pressing 2×10

4×6

  • Same weight all 4 sets; add 2.5 kg next week once all reps are clean

4×8

3×8

Pull-Ups (or lat pulldown)

Lower — Strength

Tue

Legs move more muscle and burn more calories than any other session. Your biggest single lever for both fat loss and hormonal drive.

4×6
REST3 minRPE7–8TEMPO3·0·1

  • Add 2.5–5 kg per week while bar speed stays fast

3×8
REST2 minRPE7

REST90 sRPE7

Higher reps, tighter rest, more pump. Chest, shoulders, triceps. Feel the muscle work — mind-muscle connection matters more here than raw load.

Sat + Sun · Active Recovery

Walk. Just walk.

20–40 minutes of brisk walking or easy cardio on both weekend days. Use the talk test: you can speak in full sentences, but the pace still feels purposeful. Easy movement supports the goal without digging into recovery for the next training week.

ALT TRACK

Women-focused emphasis

Same five days

The main program works for women as written. This optional track keeps the same schedule and exercise diagrams, but shifts a little more weekly work toward glutes and legs. Pick one track and progress it for at least six weeks.

No “women-only” exercises: strength training principles are the same. Start with a load that leaves about 2–3 good reps in reserve, use the listed rest, and add reps or weight only when form stays clean.
Monday · Day 01

Upper strength

Follow Day 01 as written. If pull-ups are not there yet, use the lat pulldown and progress the load.

Tuesday · Day 02

Lower + glutes

Follow Day 02, then add cable or band abductions · 2 × 15–20. Keep these controlled, not heavy.

Wednesday · Day 03

Push volume

Follow Day 03. Use 2 sets—not 4—of lateral raises if shoulder recovery limits Thursday’s pulling.

Thursday · Day 04

Pull + posture

Follow Day 04 and add face pulls · 2 × 15. Keep ribs stacked and finish each pull with the shoulder blades.

Friday · Day 05

Glute-led legs

Start with hip thrust 4 × 8, then front squat 3 × 8, split squat 3 × 10/leg, leg extension 2 × 15, and ab wheel 3 × 10.

Cycle notes

Adjust, don’t abandon

Energy and performance can vary across the menstrual cycle. On a rough day, keep the habit and reduce load or sets by 10–20%; resume normally when ready.

Food Guide

Every day

You can't out-train the kitchen. Protein at every meal, cut the oil and the liquid calories, keep the swallow portions honest. None of this means giving up Nigerian food — it means cooking it a little leaner.

Aim for a palm-sized protein portion at every meal. Protein helps with fullness and muscle retention while losing weight. Fill roughly half the plate with vegetables, keep the carbs — rice, swallow, plantain — to about a quarter, and adjust portions from the weekly trend rather than chasing perfection.

Section IIProgram Rules

Read this. It matters more than the sets.

Why 16 Weeks

At 200 lb and 6'4" you're not overweight — you carry it in the middle. Getting the belly to visibly flatten and the top abs to show means dropping roughly 15 lb of fat, and honest fat loss caps at about 1 lb per week. That's ~16 weeks. You'll notice the waist by week 4–6, a real cut by week 10–12, and top abs around week 16. A full, lower-ab six-pack usually needs another 4–8 weeks past that — so if you want the complete set, plan for 20–24 weeks and keep going.

Progressive Overload

Each week, add either weight or reps on your main lifts (the A1/A2 of each day). If you hit all prescribed reps with clean form, add 2.5 kg next session for upper body, 5 kg for lower. If you miss reps two weeks running, deload 10% and rebuild.

Nutrition Beats Training

The gym builds muscle. The kitchen strips fat. Target roughly a 500 kcal deficit, 1 g protein per lb bodyweight, and cut liquid calories — beer is the single biggest lever for a beer belly specifically. Track for two weeks minimum to know where you actually stand.

Sleep is a Training Variable

Under 6 hours and recovery, hunger, and cortisol all move against you — visceral belly fat especially. Treat 7–8 hours as a non-negotiable input, same as protein.

Cardio Placement

Steady-state cardio goes on non-lifting days or after lifting, never before. If you want more, swap one Saturday walk for intervals: 10 rounds of 30 s hard / 90 s easy on a bike or rower.

What to Track

Bodyweight (weekly average, not daily). Waist measurement at the navel (bi-weekly). Working weights on bench, squat, deadlift, and overhead press. That's it — don't over-instrument this.

When to Deload

Every 6–8 weeks, or whenever numbers stall two weeks running: drop working weights 40% for one week and keep the same movements. You'll come back stronger.

RPE — Rate of Perceived Exertion. RPE 7 = about 3 reps left in the tank. RPE 8 = 2 left. RPE 9 = 1 left. Almost never train to true failure on compounds.
AMRAP — As Many Reps As Possible, with clean form.
TEMPO 2·0·1 — 2 s down, 0 s pause, 1 s up.
